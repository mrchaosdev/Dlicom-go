# Dlicom Attack

**Hook:** Build an overpowered Dili, chain absurd skills, and purge a corrupted social network in a fast browser auto-battle roguelite.

Dlicom Attack is a browser roguelite RPG created for the Dlicom AI Game Jam. Dili fights automatically while you choose routes, draft one of three skills, and build powerful synergies.

Each 12-node run includes battles, elites, events, rest stops, and a boss. Combine 70 skills across Packet, Ban Hammer, Firewall, Viral, Moderation, and Encryption builds. Trigger Crit, Combo, Counter, Dodge, Shield, Rage, statuses, revives, and explosive Ultimates.

Fight through four chapters and defeat Spam King, Loop Phantom, Raid Master, and Null.exe. Between runs, collect Bits, upgrade 18 equipment items, open gear and skill chests, and customize Dili with four complete skins. Equipped guns, swords, hammers, armor, and modules appear in battle.

The game supports desktop and mobile, mouse and touch controls, local saves, original music, and AI-assisted original artwork. No account, wallet, payment, or download is required.
